## Talk on John Chrysostom

John Chrysostom (347-407 AD) - "Golden Mouth"

Born in Antioch, studied rhetoric and philosophy. Became a priest and later the Archbishop of Constantinople.

Known for his eloquent preaching and public speaking, he was a strong advocate for the poor and criticized the excesses of the wealthy.

His writings include homilies and letters, many of which are still studied today.

Chrysostom's legacy includes his influence on Christian liturgy and his role in the development of monasticism in the East.

He was eventually exiled due to his outspoken criticism of the imperial court, but his teachings continued to have a lasting impact on the church.

Discussion about Priests forgiving sins and the gradual punishment for different sins.

The power doesn't come from the person, but from the office given by Christ to the church.

His goal in his book on priesthood: to encourage priests to live a holy life and to fulfill their duties with dedication and humility. Becoming a priest was a way for power in Chrysostom's time, but he wanted to change that mindset.

Sober people's up to become priests.

What is the role of a priest? To equip the church, to build the church. In this way the priest gives glory to God.

You need a thousand eyes to watch over the sheep. The priest needs to be vigilant and attentive to the needs of the congregation.
