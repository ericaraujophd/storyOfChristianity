Mark Noll, Turning Points (quote on monks)

It took two centuries for the reformed to understand how to do missions. The monks used to do it, and during the reformation the monks were gone. So the reformers had to reinvent missions.

Dietrich Bonhoeffer, Life Together (quote on community)

Monks were the professional missionaries.

Papacy took an active role in missions. When papacy is so in control of things, it can be a problem.

The temptation of imposing a culture along with the gospel. The papacy doesn't do a good indigenization job.

Mass conversions were common. European tribes would convert en masse when their leader converted.

Report of the conversion of the Vikings (quote) - the leader discerning for two days before declaring conversion of the whole tribe.

Trial by ordeal to discern which god is real.

Example of Boniface cutting down the Donar Oak to show the power of the Christian God over the pagan gods. He uses the tree to make a chapel for St. Peter.

The missions start from Ireland and Britain and move east and south.
