During this section we read the writings from and about a few important martyrs of the early church. Here is a list of some of them:

## Ignatius of Antioch

(ignatius_of_antioch)=
Ignatius of Antioch (c. 35–107 AD) was an early Christian bishop and martyr. He is known for his letters, which he wrote while being transported to Rome for execution. In these letters, Ignatius emphasized the importance of church unity, the role of bishops, and the significance of the Eucharist. His writings provide valuable insights into early Christian theology and ecclesiology.

- [Letter to the Magnesians](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/richardson/fathers.vi.ii.iii.ii.html)
- [Letter to the Romans](https://ccel.org/ccel/richardson/fathers/fathers.vi.ii.iii.iv.html)
- [Letter to the Smyrnaeans](https://ccel.org/ccel/richardson/fathers/fathers.vi.ii.iii.vi.html)

## Saint Perpetua and Felicitas

(perpetua_and_felicitas)=
Saint Perpetua and Felicitas were early Christian martyrs who lived in Carthage in the 3rd century AD. Perpetua was a noblewoman, and Felicitas was her slave. Both women were arrested for their Christian faith and refused to renounce it. They were executed in the arena, and their story has been preserved in a diary written by Perpetua herself (allegedly), which provides a rare firsthand account of early Christian martyrdom.

- [The Martyrdom of Saints Perpetua and Felicitas](../documents/The_Martyrdom_of_Saints_Perpetua_and_Felicitas.pdf)
